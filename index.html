The gap

6.3%*

*Provincial budget

Psychiatric care is underfunded across Canada.

That’s the average share of provincial and territorial health budgets that goes to psychiatric care. No other branch of medicine would be asked to run on this.

Source: Canadian Mental Health Association, The State of Mental Health in Canada 2024

Our mission

To make psychiatric care in Canada as equitable, well-funded and humane as the rest of medicine.

  1. 1 in 10

    01 / 03

    ER reform

    One in ten people who come to the ER for mental health or substance use care are back four or more times in a year. We want one standard of psychiatric emergency care in every ER, city or small town.

    Source: Canadian Institute for Health Information (2024)CIHR, 2024

  2. 3%

    02 / 03

    Research funding

    Research on mental illness receives about 3% of federal health research investment. We’re pushing for dedicated, sustained funding for psychiatric research.

    Source: Canadian Journal of Psychiatry (2023), CIHR data 2016–2021CJP, 2023

  3. 40%

    03 / 03

    Humane care at the front line

    Four in ten people living with a psychiatric or substance use disorder report stigma when getting healthcare. Everyone should leave an ER or psychiatric service feeling they were treated with dignity.

The question we ask of every policy

Would we accept this anywhere else in medicine?

Every budget. Every wait time. Every standard of care. If the answer is no, it’s not good enough for psychiatry either.

Who we are

Built by someone who knows how the system works.

Meghan White has spent 15 years inside Canadian healthcare and has changed policy at every level of government, from city hall to Parliament.

A four-time women’s health founder, national board executive and communications leader, she helped secure Canada’s $25 million Menstrual Equity Fund and championed the national strategy for rare disease drugs.

She is also a psychiatric patient.

She knows this system from the boardroom and from the waiting room. PCCC is built on both.

“Nobody should have to acquire an expertise in institutional failure while waiting for care. A healthcare system is a public promise. We all have a responsibility to insist that it is kept.”

Meghan White, Founder
